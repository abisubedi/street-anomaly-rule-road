Privacy Policy

Rule Road by Street Anomaly · Last updated October 18, 2026

The short version

Rule Road does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your device

  • The stars you earn in each town
  • Whether you closed the "install" tip

These are saved only in your own browser or app storage. They never leave your device, and clearing the app's data deletes them.

Outside services

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any website, your device's IP address is visible to Google Fonts and to our host, Netlify, when files are downloaded. We do not receive or keep that information. Links to YouTube open YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is family-friendly and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
