Privacy Policy

Rule Road by Street Anomaly · Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version

Rule Road does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your device

  • The stars you earn, your daily streak and your settings
  • The name you type for a challenge link, if you type one

These are saved only in your own browser or app storage. Clearing the app's data deletes them.

No microphone, no recordings

Rule Road never asks for your microphone or camera. Every town is played by touch, mouse or keyboard.

Sharing

A result card or challenge link is only shared when you press Share, and only with the people you send it to. A challenge link holds only the town, your stars and the name you typed.

Outside services

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, your device's IP address is visible to them when files are downloaded. We do not receive or keep that information.

Children

The game is family-friendly and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact email on the Rule Road store listing.
